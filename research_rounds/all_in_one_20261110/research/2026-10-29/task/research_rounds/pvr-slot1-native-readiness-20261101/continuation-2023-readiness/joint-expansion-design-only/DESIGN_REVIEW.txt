Earned stock expansion: ONE design hypothesis, not implemented or backtested
2026-11-01. Supersedes the earlier incomplete suggestion to require an old
probe-pool holding before every confirmed order. No new indicator or account
path is authorized. All figures below are research fixtures, not user limits.

The concrete contradiction
An active expanded industry can already hold12% NAV in confirmed positions.
Original probe admission checks ALL industry commitments against2% NAV, so a
new stock cannot use probe even if global probe is empty. If stock proof were
then required for its first confirmed order, neither route would remain. This
would starve discovery mechanically, not distinguish good from bad trends.
The existing first confirmed mobile order can legally be2% NAV; this route
cannot be silently removed while claiming that only promotion changed.

ONE minimal coherent hypothesis
Keep first discovery possible under the ORIGINAL routes. Add only a stock-
campaign validation condition to grow its aggregate commitment above the
original2% mobile target. A trial is an action state, not a pool or a role.
Before proof, total stock holdings plus outstanding buy commitments may not
receive new allocation above2% of its frozen decision NAV. With current proof
and a fresh original stock CP1, it may seek the original per-order target and
original10% total stock cap. All original resource constraints still decide
the actual quantity. This repurposes the existing2% target as an aggregate
pre-proof ceiling: an economic research change, not a sizing bugfix.
This is gross stock capital, as in the existing capital resource view; fees
remain separately reserved and charged under original cash/NAV rules.
An unproved small core may still receive an original fresh mobile request
within the aggregate2% trial ceiling. This is not N1's prohibition of all
mobile orders until a seed has been promoted.

Four axes, kept separate
1 Industry attempt/stage: original active probe/confirmed/expanded permits
  original combined industry capital2/10/20%; failure revokes new permission
  and requests buy cancellation, never sells all existing stocks by itself.
2 Stock evidence: real filled campaign, original effective protective lines,
  economic cash-flow ledger and fresh stock CP1 validate additional capital.
  Industry expanded alone or mark-to-market profit is not stock proof.
3 Role/protection: original I core keeps its original I/E0 line and exits;
  mobile keeps original CP1-mobile line and exits. No relabeling or replacing
  a far core line with a near CP1 candidate to manufacture proof. Same-stock
  core-first reservation semantics remain, including zero-fill suppression.
4 Pool: first trial may be probe OR confirmed if the original route permits.
  It is not a new pool. Probe10% capital/.5% nominal risk and its combined
  industry2% test remain. Confirmed60%, industry nominal risk1%, stock10%,
  cash/fees, lot, Q and ACT remain. Reference PVR/Bdiag has NO account3%
  admission test; this design does not quietly restore it or invent a new
  global risk ceiling. Confirmed-pool trial risk is not charged to probe;
  that is already true of the original first confirmed-mobile opportunity.
  Stock proof never overrides industry stage, Q, ACT or a failed original
  route. These conditions are jointly necessary, not substitute permissions.

Campaign and proof accounting (design definition, not new computed data)
A stock campaign starts only on an actual fill after actual zero holdings
and completion of all old buy/sell responsibilities. It ends on actual full
flat, not on an industry attempt change or exit request. Partial exits and
closed layers while other layers remain stay in the SAME campaign.

PROOF CLOCK, fixed before any coverage analysis
Use the LAST ACCEPTED line at the completed signal close, after the ORIGINAL
old-line exit check and then the original permitted line update. It becomes
eligible for proof at the NEXT observation/open. It cannot stop the preceding
bar retroactively. This follows the existing update/effect clock, not a choice
of whichever clock retains a selected winner. A previous-observation-only
proof is a different contract and is not an alternative to test in parallel.
Shanghai Mar20's+1032.39 uses that close's new accepted line; using Mar17's
line would instead give-200.64. Both are recorded, neither is concealed.
The buy-day closing G1 cold readings were unavailable at that day's earlier
OPEN and cannot be used to reject those original entries retrospectively.

At that completed decision close, freeze the line identities and values,
factor domain, campaign accounting and decision NAV for next-open intents:
F = campaign actual trading cash flows, net of actual buy/sell fees
    + campaign recognized net dividends, paid or receivable counted ONCE
    + sum(live layer quantity * that layer's real effective protective price)
    - pending-buy nominal downside from reserved limit to its frozen floor
    - its separately reserved buy fees.
No spendable cash is substituted for trading cash flows. This includes all
realized partial losses and fees within the campaign; closed gains are not
counted again as a live position. Pending orders cannot earn positive proof.
Lines, quantities and costs use the same actual split/adjustment domain.
Core uses its actual original I/E0 reference, mobile its actual CP1 line.
An already breached old line or pending exit cannot be rescued by a new CP1.

Opening revalidation, downward only
Keep the original preview rejection when known opening raw price is at/below
the NEW order's frozen line. Also require each EXISTING live campaign layer's
accepted line to remain unbreached by the known opening price, in the same
post-action factor domain. If any is breached, withdraw all same-stock NEW
buy permission at that open; do not invent an earlier sell or a new line.
Original holdings and exits remain governed by original close/exit rules.
This existing-layer buy veto is an explicit proposed economic permission
change; the current engine does NOT already enforce it for every held line.
For proof valuation at the open, bound each accepted line by the lower of
that line and known open*(1-original sell slippage). Proof may only stay the
same or weaken versus the frozen close view, never be earned from a gap up.
This uses known price plus the unchanged model price adjustment, NOT a call
to market.window, whose daily H/L/volume/close availability is not known at
the open. The proxy bound still does not establish actual executable liquidity.
Unknown opening quote, invalid factor or pending exit blocks new permission.
No current-day closing G1/CP1, later line, or future OHLC validity enters proof.

F>0 is a validation condition for the next fresh-CP1 risk request, NOT cash,
NOT a transferable coupon and NOT a promise of executable net stop proceeds.
Future sell fees/slippage and gaps can turn this nominal surplus into loss.
It is deliberately not a claim that protected profit fully funds the new
order's possible loss. Actual cash and original mark-to-floor risk checks
remain binding even when F is positive.
It is account-conditioned validation, not a new market-strength indicator:
the same market receipt can qualify differently at different actual costs.

Proof is reevaluated before every new request over the2% trial ceiling; it is
not a permanent promoted label. One original order per stock, fresh canonical
CP1 consumed once only after final legal routing/sizing/reservation. The
reservation enters commitments AND the next proof view before another request;
no duplicate grant or credit is minted from the same F/receipt. A request can
make F nonpositive; then further expansion stops until fresh evidence and
current proof return. Existing holdings above2% are not force-sold or washed
into a new trial allowance. Passive price growth neither earns proof alone
nor causes forced selling.

When genuinely flat with no pending responsibilities, a new fresh original
signal may start another trial without recovering ALL historical stock losses.
Original loss/fee records persist across campaigns/E0/industry attempts. This
avoids C1-like historical hysteresis while leaving repeated false-start damage
visible. A remaining old core or unacknowledged order prevents campaign reset.

First trial -> proof -> additional risk -> exit -> restart
Flat fresh original I/core or stock CP1/mobile may request the original order,
subject to the new stock aggregate trial ceiling and all original routes.
In a confirmed industry already above2%, a fresh stock CP1 can therefore still
use a first confirmed-mobile trial if original confirmed capacity is available.
Core-only opportunities still cannot borrow a mobile route without CP1.
Once actual campaign F>0, later fresh CP1 can seek more original mobile risk.
Industry migration remains original best effort/coupled; moving pool labels
does not change cash, role, floor, stock commitment or F. Migration failure
can still block a confirmed route; this hypothesis does not include SD.
Original protection/I/ACT determines sell request and legal fill. Eligibility
loss cancels additional risk only; no earlier refund or imaginary lower risk.

Strong counterexamples / failure boundaries, not a parameter menu
A. Strong industry12% NAV held in confirmed, industry cap20%, global space:
  old probe route rejects its first new stock (combined industry>2%). A fresh
  CP1 first trial of at most2% stock can still seek confirmed under original
  rules. If confirmed/global/sector risk is actually full, it still cannot
  enter. The design prevents new logical starvation, not all missed stocks.
B. First false breakout: the first confirmed2% trial can still lose at its
  original floor, and a gap can lose more. Many fresh campaigns may still
  accumulate substantial losses. This does not solve the initial-entry loss
  cohort or promise a hard drawdown/loss ceiling.
C. Continuous winner with a far original core line: mark price can double
  while F stays negative, leaving it at trial size. No borrowing the mobile
  line is allowed. This can falsify the architecture's ability to scale a
  winner before the main move; it is not an irrelevant edge case.
D. Normal pullback and reentry: Shanghai's early G1 cold is not a new exit.
  After actual full flat, another fresh trial remains possible. Old huge
  stock gains cannot permanently authorize a new failing campaign; conversely
  old failed campaigns cannot require lifelong recovery before a new trial.
E. Gap/fees/lot: a positive nominal F does not assure a safe stop fill. A new
  opening gap may require shrinking the original frozen quantity;100/200-share
  initial minimum and later increments remain dated native rules. No R1,
  automatic roundup or fee forgiveness. A profitable one-cent F may be a poor
  validation signal; do not tune a buffer using observed winners to fix it.
  Mandatory source-backed gap example: an old mobile lot has line100, its
  next opening price95, while a fresh CP1 order has a lower frozen line80.
  Current LR.preview sees95>80 and does not reject on the old100 line; current
  held nominal risk max(mark-floor,0) can be0. The proposed opening proof must
  be invalid and no new stock buy admitted, not count100 as realizable wealth.
  A pending exit also blocks even if the line valuation would be positive.
  The example is a written preflight requirement, not an executed policy test.
F. Capacity/partial/cancel: original combined sector holdings, old attempts,
  pending buys and cancel-pending quantities count. Unfilled sell proceeds
  cannot be spent. A partial fill cannot restart a campaign or issue proof
  repeatedly. All capital/risk is rechecked serially; phase names release none.

Relation to actual N1 (source inventory already complete)
N1: one filled seed K=(buy gross+buy fees)/shares*factor. A post-buy NEW F or
CP1 candidate strictly>K promotes that seed. Core then stops following new
F/CP1 and retains bridge/I protection; mobile requires that core, new CP1 and
a price above last actual buy. It also changed minlot allocation and reentry.
This proposal does NOT change holding roles/exit responsibilities or adopt its
minlot exceptions/PVR>50/current-G1 conditions. Proof uses whole current
campaign, including closed partial losses and pending liabilities; it is not
a fresh candidate>K or a one-time latched seed promotion. First confirmed
trial remains possible under the90-industry pool contract. It is a distinct
joint contract; old N1 results neither prove nor refute it.

Prefinancial coverage plan ONLY (requires later authorization to execute)
Use existing fixed2022, own continuous2023 and2025Jul..2026Jun PVR books/caches.
No new labels, indicators, account replay or state-restorer. Enumerate ALL
original candidate/intents, including core-only, NA PVR, no fills and ACT
paused, before looking at actual profitability. Deduplicate canonical stock
CP1 and distinguish actual first/add/flat reentry, role and pool.
Campaign history must start at its actual first fill, including carried
campaigns; do not reanchor at a quarter/year boundary or mark old holdings
proved without source evidence. Preserve unknown where history is incomplete.
The above post-close/next-open clock and downward-only gap revalidation are
fixed before coverage; do not revise them after seeing affected cohort returns.
For each saved decision classify flat first trial, live unproved at/below2%,
live unproved above2% passively, currently proved, exit/pending, or unknown.
Keep original industry total commitments so first confirmed trials in an
industry already>2% are explicitly counted. Never infer exact opening owner
states from previous close or call a permitted request an executable trade.
Mark missing native line/cost/receipt timing unknown; do not impute a nearer
floor or rebuild a broad input platform. Cash/fee/split/pending arithmetic
must reconcile to saved ledgers; old loss ledger must remain unchanged.

Inspect the fixed largest-gross-loss case plus all winners/losers, especially
continuous core winners and mobile winners, not only Yongding/Shanghai.
Report nonzero permission changes, unaffected/unknown counts, stock/date
concentration, trial capital and nominal risk, closed positive/negative/tail
and open contribution of observed affected cohorts. These are mechanism
coverage/accounting, NOT counterfactual PnL or a new winning gate.
The design fails before finance if proof cannot be causally reconstructed,
if first-trial paths are stranded by its own new rules, if the initial2%
ceiling is bypassed by role/pool/attempt changes, or if its actual mechanism
cannot express scaling a continuous winner without a pullback. Broader
opportunity/false-exit tradeoffs must be exposed, not silently patched.
Only after source/clock/accounting and positive+negative controls pass should
root decide whether ONE bounded native comparison is informative. No numeric
optimization, risk-budget sweep, algorithm implementation or finance is
authorized by this document.
