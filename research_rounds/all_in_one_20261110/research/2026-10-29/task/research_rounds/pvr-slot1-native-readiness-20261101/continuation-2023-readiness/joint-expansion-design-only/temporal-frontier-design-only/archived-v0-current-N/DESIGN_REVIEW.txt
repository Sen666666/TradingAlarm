T1: temporal frontier permission with conserved trial capacity -- DESIGN ONLY
2026-11-01. ONE candidate for review. Not implementation/coverage/run approval.
Universal financing-B remains STOPPED; no protected-principal requirement here.

Question and claim
Can distributed NEW price-frontier progress qualify fresh CP1 risk expansion,
while initial uncertainty stays in a shared, limited trial pool? The proposed
index measures distribution of positive progress, NOT independent samples,
probability, trend quality or guaranteed capital preservation. Real accepted
loss remains governed by actual protection and unchanged hard resources.

1. Exact proposed market evidence (no calculation has been executed)
Use the original causal price/amount source and exact original PVR DAILY
features, not PVR aggregate score and not CP1's lower-bounded frozen z:
 r_t=log(C_t/C_previous_valid),
 s_t=max(1.4826*median(previous63 absolute log returns),1e-8),
 A_t=median(previous21 amounts),
 u_t=tanh(r_t/s_t), b_t=tanh(log(amount_t/A_t)),
 m_t=1+.5*abs(u_t)*b_t.
Current close/amount enter only after that observation has completed; s/A
exclude it. Original valid blocks/source resets apply; missing/nonpositive
amount or invalid price/source cannot be filled as zero or neutral evidence.
The DAILY features prepare before the original 85-observation aggregate score:
that difference is explicit, not an accidental bypass of PVR-score readiness.

At each valid, feature-ready observation in a defined stock market attempt:
 a_t=tanh(max(0,log(C_t)-F_previous)/s_t)*m_t,
 F_t=max(F_previous,log(C_t)).
F is an attempt-wide maximum, not CP1's receipt-reset anchor, purchase cost,
all-time stock high, or industry price. Equal closes and recoveries below F
contribute zero. Keep the last min(21,attempt_length) such observations,
including valid zero-progress days. No invented full21-day startup wait.
 N=(sum a)^2/sum(a^2); sum(a^2)=0 means no progress/proof, not NA division.
Five is one fixed research hypothesis, not an optimized/user risk ceiling.
N>=5 is the proposed current permission state; it is not consumed by orders
or CP1 receipts. Falling below5 blocks future expansion; it does not sell or
reclassify an existing position. No other pace/return threshold is added.

2. Causal market attempt and reset order -- independent of money
All identities include canonical stock/source/causal starting observation;
stock market attempt != stock funded campaign != industry stage attempt.
First process source/availability and original I validity. I invalid ends the
market attempt; unknown evidence withdraws new permission without inventing
an exit. A new ready, I-valid observation initializes SEEK at current close,
queue empty/a=0. No earlier rows are backfilled into the new attempt.
While I valid, an ORIGINAL negative E0 event resets SEEK at current close,
clears F and queue, and makes proof false that observation even if another
signal is present. While SEEK, a strictly new low resets anchor/F/queue again
using that observation and credits no advance. This is the original CP1 SEEK
lower-anchor rule, not a purchase-price reset.
First original CP1 receipt formed wholly after this market reset transitions
SEEK to UP. KEEP accumulated a/F on that receipt. Every later CP1 receipt also
keeps them. In UP, a small pullback does not lower F; only original negative
E0/I/source resets restart it. A later recovery can restart BELOW an old peak
when actual negative/reset evidence occurred; no hard all-time-high gate.
Do not reset on G1 grade alone, a failed order, sell, quarter, ACT phase reset,
receipt consumption or a portfolio loss. Those are different clocks.
If original CP1 formation began before a newer source/market reset, it cannot
validate this attempt's confirmed permission. Signal output itself is unchanged.
At completed signal close freeze N, F/attempt, original fresh CP1 ID and the
full existing priority key. Next-open execution cannot read that day's close,
amount, G1/E0 or future CP1 to recreate proof. Missing lineage remains unknown.
An original core first trial can still occur without CP1; NEW expansion always
needs an original fresh, legal, not-previously-consumed CP1 plus current proof,
original Q/ACT and industry permission. No proof-only standing/GTC order.

4. Nonredundancy and what is NOT yet established
G1 already reads 5/21 pace,21/63 structure and fresh frontier reasons. PVR is
weighted SIGNED daily evidence over21, CP1 accumulates frontier displacement
and m-credit but resets after every receipt. T1 adds concentration of NEW
frontier advances across time without receipt reset; it shares price,scale,
amount and several frontier ideas. It is not independent corroboration and
might add no useful information beyond these original signals.
The user's original E0 signal-density/persistence hypothesis has NOT been
refuted by previous CP1-repeat or weekly opportunity-quality diagnostics.
T1 does not count E0 event density and is not a test of that exact hypothesis.
N rescales away total amplitude: five equally tiny advances also yield5.
Original fresh CP1 supplies a displacement requirement, not proof of medium
trend. CP1 interval and N's recent21 interval may differ. N can remain>=5 for
several later receipts: each new order does not earn five extra independent
days. No current-frontier-only add gate is silently introduced to fix this.

5. Matched manual counterexamples -- not executed indicator/unit tests
- Same ready scale and amount, same total log advance5s: one-day advance5s
  then flat gives one nonzero a and N=1; five advances of s on different days
  give five equal a and N=5. Both use the same endpoint. Five unequal advances
  generally give N<5; five effective days is not five literal up days.
- Falling highs/lows followed by a distributed six-day bounce can produce
  N>=5 and fresh CP1 while original far-line I remains active. After negative
  reset, the bounce can qualify BELOW the old high. T1 does NOT solve that
  countertrend failure; if exclusion of these bounces is mandatory, reject T1
  rather than adding another hidden direction gate. Real trial/sector risk
  absorbs accepted mistakes, with no hard cumulative-loss guarantee.
- A normal pullback without original negative/reset keeps F. Recovery below
  F contributes0; old positive evidence ages out, possibly delaying valid
  winner adds until new frontiers. A pullback large enough for original E0
  negative clears proof; next recovery must earn it again. Existing core/mobile
  exits still act independently and can cause false exits/reentries.
- Failed attempt/new low then distributed new trend below former peak can
  requalify after original reset. Same initial prefix produces identical
  decisions before failure/winner branches separate; no hindsight reset.
- Monotonic core with unchanged far E0 floor can reach N>=5 and a fresh CP1;
  it can expand if actual old/new risk and cash allow. No B-style deadlock.
  A valid minimum order may still be unaffordable, old trial transfer may not
  fit60%, or proof may mature only after the main move. No R1/ceiling increase.
- A proved campaign reset while holding20% cannot obtain a fresh2% trial;
  existing commitments remain. Unproved trial stocks across industries cannot
  fill60%: they must share10%/.5% until actually proved and legally migrated.

Verdict
One coherent candidate is specified, with explicit stock-scoped migration and
industry-total-cap changes. It is NOT implementation-ready as a one-line gate,
not proved superior, and not permission to run coverage or financial paths.
Its falsifiable claim is distributed-vs-impulsive market permission usable
under finite trial capacity, not risk-free expansion or elimination of bearish
rebounds. Strongest unresolved semantic cost is resetting good pullbacks versus
allowing distributed bearish recoveries; no new filter is selected here.
If root continues, only the unchanged-source matched manual logic and native
conservation/clock cases above should be designed/approved first. No new scale,
window, threshold grid, feature cache, indicator or account was calculated.
