A versus B
A asks whether positive pre-add nominal protected campaign PnL is useful as
an expansion qualification. A expressly does not promise that profit pays the
incremental risk. B=1000 and new-order downside+fees=4000 may pass A, yet leave
the full campaign at-3000 at stated floors. That is coherent for predictive
permission, but is NOT risk-financed expansion.

Recommend B because its accounting claim is falsifiable without a fitted risk
threshold: above the unchanged aggregate stock2% trial permission, an order
may be admitted only if FULL current-campaign floor-valued net PnL remains
>=0 after the order and ALL other commitments/known modeled buy+exit fees.
There is no extra R0 or optimized cushion. B is NOT presumed to improve returns;
it can materially delay or prevent winner participation. A will not be run as
another variant. Only B is the current proposal if root accepts this review.

Trial and industry/pool axes, unchanged from the joint design
Trial is a stock action state, not the probe pool. A flat fresh CP1 stock in a
confirmed/expanded industry may still seek its original first confirmed trial
even when total industry commitments already exceed2%. The original probe
route still tests ALL industry commitments against2%; it is not relaxed.
First/additional trial allocation may remain within aggregate stock2% of the
original frozen decision NAV without satisfying the financed-expansion test.
Above2%, the FULL stock-campaign test applies, not just the portion above2%.
Original I core risk sizing, mobile2% per-order target, stock10%, industry
2/10/20 capital, industry risk1%, probe10% capital/.5% risk, confirmed60%,
cash plus original cash reserve, fees, ACT, Q, dated lot/minimum/step, role
priority, pool routing and migration coupling all remain independent checks.
Reference Bdiag/PVR has no account3% admission ceiling; B does not add one.
The2% trial ceiling is a research economic policy, not a user risk preference.
Passive growth above2% is not sold; it simply does not grant unfunded new risk.

Campaign scope and source of protected credit
A campaign begins with an actual fill after actual full flat and completion
of old pending buy/sell/cancel responsibilities. It includes every live and
closed partial layer until actual full flat. Industry attempts/E0 calendar
boundaries alone do not restart its money history. Actual trading cash flows
include all actual buy and sell fees and all realized partial gains/losses.
Recognized net dividends are included once; unpaid receivables are economic
equity, never spendable cash. Separate original cash constraints still apply.
After genuine full flat, a fresh original signal may start another trial
without repaying all career stock losses. The global historical loss/fee
ledger never resets. Old profitable campaigns cannot finance a new campaign's
proof; this avoids both a permanent permission and C1-like lifetime lockout.

Clock: unchanged last accepted completed signal-close line
First apply original old-line/I/ACT exit decisions. Only unexited layers may
accept original permitted line increases. Freeze the last accepted lines at
that COMPLETED signal close for the NEXT observation/open. No same-bar
retroactive stop or buy-day closing G1 enters the earlier opening admission.
Shanghai+1032.39 used Mar20's new accepted line; Mar17's line gives-200.64.
Those are different clocks, disclosed before coverage. The clock follows
original update/effect semantics; it is not selected to retain that winner.
Core uses its actual I/E0 reference, mobile its actual CP1 line, in the same
current split/adjustment domain. There is no reassignment of a nearer line.

Opening safeguards from the prior design remain
Original preview must still reject open at/below the new intent's own floor.
If the known opening price breaches ANY live campaign layer's accepted line,
withdraw same-stock new-buy permission; do not fabricate an early sell.
Pending exit or unknown opening quote/factor blocks permission. This added
held-line buy veto is a proposed policy condition, not an existing guard.
Value projected exits at accepted raw floor*(1-original sell slippage), capped
by known open*(1-original sell slippage) when revalidating at the open. Proof
can only weaken from the frozen close view, not be earned from a gap up.
Use known opening price only, not market.window/HLCV future availability.
This sell-price adjustment now enters B's explicit exit-cost accounting; the
underlying accepted-line clock is unchanged. It does not assert real liquidity.

Precise full-campaign valuation, no cash or double-credit fiction
Let C be actual campaign trading cash flows net of actual fees. Let D be its
recognized net dividends. Neither includes an unfilled future sell receipt.
For each live remaining quantity, use its real accepted protective reference
and the modeled sell-price adjustment above. Pending buys contribute their
remaining quantity at their frozen floor and debit their remaining principal
budget PLUS separately reserved remaining buy fees. Actual partial fills are
already in C and live quantity; only their unfilled remainder is a placeholder.
For the proposed order q, debit q*known modeled buy price plus the native
full-order buy fee ONCE, and add q*its own original frozen protective reference
with the same modeled sell-price adjustment.

Subtract projected future sell fees for ALL live/pending/proposed quantities.
For this nominal budget, group by stock, exit role/rule and exact accepted
protective reference; quantities sharing that reference share one hypothetical
sell order's minimum commission. Different references/exit rules are separate
projected orders. No new rounding/tolerance matching of lines is introduced.
Use the unchanged fee schedule available at the decision/open, including dated
stamp/handling/regulatory/transfer/brokerage and original minimum/rounding.
This is a deliberately conservative grouping assumption relative to possible
future coalesced exits, not a forecast of actual future orders or fee dates.
Already paid sell fees remain in C; never subtract them again. With an actual
pending exit there is no new admission, so its old accrued fee cannot be
recounted as a new credit or a fictitious fresh full-order charge.

V(q) = C + D - pending principal - remaining pending buy fees
       - proposed buy gross(q) - proposed buy fee(q)
       + projected gross exit value of ALL live/pending/proposed shares
       - projected remaining sell fees of those shares.

For any order crossing the trial capital ceiling, require V(q)>=0 for the
FULL campaign. This is not pre-add V(0)>0, not a test on the new layer alone,
and not a test only on excess shares beyond2%. Realized profits from closed
partial layers can finance risk only within their still-open campaign.
The original cash/marked capital/nominal-risk constraints ALSO apply; protected
credit is never added to cash or removed from mark-to-floor giveback risk.
An exact V=0 is allowed; no hidden spare-credit threshold is added.

Reservations consume the projection immediately, before any later request.
On fill the pending placeholder is replaced ONCE by actual cash/quantity/fees;
on cancellation it stays until native acknowledgement. No grant is issued,
no historical receipt is revived, no new pool created. A new request needs its
own fresh canonical CP1 and the current full campaign valuation. Core-first
and one actual order per stock remain. Failed sizing/reservation consumes no
new CP1 credit. Cash/role/pool/campaign lineage must reconcile before finance.

Exact quantity clipping specification (math only; not policy code)
For each originally eligible route, consider only the original dated legal
quantities q=minimum+k*step, within its frozen original target/share/gross
cap and ALL original opening constraints. q=0 means no order.
Keep q if either:
  (i) post-order total stock gross commitment <=2% frozen decision NAV, OR
  (ii) full V(q)>=0.
Take the largest such legal q. Then retain the original maximum-quantity
route comparison/tie rule; do not rerank stocks or repick a role after success.
Both planning preview and final admission must use the SAME full test.
If one legal minimum fails both (i) and (ii), reject; do not round upward,
borrow probe or invoke R1. Only reduce the original frozen quantity on an
opening gap; a lower price cannot create a larger original share authorization.
All pending commitments count in stock/trial/global/industry views and in V.

Research meaning and next boundary
B tests risk-funded stock expansion, not merely predictive permission or a
new strength indicator. Its cash-flow constraint is mechanically stronger;
that is not evidence of better returns or better fat-tail capture. Different
entry costs still imply different permission under identical market evidence.
The fee grouping and original close-line clock are fixed here before coverage,
not chosen after inspecting which winners survive.
Any later authorized readiness study must report first trials, full/partial
original target, minlot-blocked credit, role/pool, pending and unknowns across
ALL saved decisions. Include strong continuous core/mobile winners, false
breakouts, past partial losses, genuine flat reentry, gaps and cash/cap-full
negative controls. No new account replay or future-price optimization.
If credit/fee/line history cannot be causally reconstructed, or B structurally
prevents the intended winner expansion, report that limitation/falsification;
do not tune R0, floor, trial size or fees to make examples pass.
Root must adjudicate this recommendation before any coverage calculation,
policy implementation or financial path. Only this ONE B contract is proposed;
the archived A is not a second experimental arm or a hidden fallback.
