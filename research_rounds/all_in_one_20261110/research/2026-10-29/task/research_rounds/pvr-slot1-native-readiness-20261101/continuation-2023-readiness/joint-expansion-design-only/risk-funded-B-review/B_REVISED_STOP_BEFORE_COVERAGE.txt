Clarified B lifecycle (needed to assess the claim, NOT approved to implement)
1 Initial trial is ONE original first-order authorization, capped at2% frozen
  decision NAV and original risk/lot/cash/industry/pool limits. It is NOT a
  daily mark-weight refill. First confirmed mobile remains possible in a
  strong industry already>2%, if the original confirmed route/capacity permits.
  Probe still checks ALL industry commitments<=2%; no new pool is created.
2 A campaign starts only on actual first fill, not zero-fill reservation.
  Partial fills can complete ONLY that original first order's remaining
  quantity/gross/fee authorization and original deadline. Unused or cancelled
  allowance cannot be revived. EVERY subsequent fresh order is expansion
  and must pass full B, even while stock weight remains below2%.
3 Actual full flat and no active buy/sell/cancel responsibilities ends it.
  Unpaid receivables do not keep a campaign open forever. A new original legal
  fresh signal can start a new trial without recovering all historical stock
  loss; original portfolio/campaign loss/fee history never resets.
4 Only current-campaign CASH-CONFIRMED income enters proof: actual settled
  net sale receipts and cash-paid attributed dividends. Filled buy costs/fees
  and obligations count immediately. Unsettled sale/dividend receivables stay
  separate portfolio assets, excluded from B. Later OLD-campaign payments do
  not become NEW-campaign credit, though actual global cash may fund trials.
5 All live and closed partial layers belong to their same still-open campaign;
  retain realized partial losses and actual fees. No mark-profit credit.

V(q) = current-campaign settled NET SALE RECEIPTS (actual sell fees deducted)
       + cash-paid current-campaign net dividends
       - cumulative filled BUY gross/fees and other known unpaid costs once
       - remaining pending-buy principal and separately reserved buy fees
       - new q*modeled buy price and one native aggregate new buy fee
       + projected exits of ALL live/pending/new quantities at their REAL lines
       - projected remaining sell fees of ALL those quantities.
Use original dated fee/minimum/rounding functions. Same-role exact-line exit
references share a projected fee; different references are conservatively
separate. Past paid fees are already in cash flows and cannot be charged twice.
Partial buys replace only their pending remainder once; cancel release waits
for ack. Actual pending exit blocks new buys, not a manufactured credit.
All original capital, cash/reserve, risk, ACT, Q, role and route rules ALSO
apply. Reference Bdiag/PVR does not regain a hidden account3% risk ceiling.

Every post-initial order needs V(q)>=0 for the FULL campaign, not merely the
excess above2% and not positive V(0). Choose the largest ORIGINAL legal
minimum+k*step within frozen target/share/gross caps and original hard limits
that passes V(q)>=0. q=0 means no order. No R0, roundup, revived unused trial
or mark-drop refill; no greater authorization after a cheaper opening.
Reservations enter V before another request; fresh canonical CP1 remains
single-use and original core-first/one-stock-order/route tie rules remain.

Clock unchanged: old-line check first, then original accepted signal-close
update, proof usable NEXT observation/open only. No retroactive bar stop or
buy-day closing G1 at that earlier OPEN. At open, unknown price/factor/pending
exit or gap through ANY held line withdraws new-buy permission, not old exit
duty. Projected sale value uses original floor minus original sell slippage,
capped by known open with that adjustment; no market.window/HLCV lookahead.
This is nominal projected financing, not guaranteed stop liquidity, future
taxes, realized capital preservation or a hard DD ceiling.

STRUCTURAL COUNTEREXAMPLE: B cannot expand a continuous CORE winner
Actual source conditions, not a model execution:
(a) While E0 direction=UP, a new high sets extreme=t; t>extreme is then false.
  A monotone rise creates no new swing event. Insufficient reversals also do
  not switch the event direction. A fresh positive swing requires a down leg.
(b) Active E0 floor increases only on a positive event's confirmed pivot.
  I entry-only projection follows/maxes that base floor, with no cold exit.
(c) Native core copies facts.floor(I) into last_core_line then CONTINUES,
  skipping CP1 ratchets. The budget sync uses that actual core line. Fresh
  higher CP1 candidates do NOT protect core. Core has no regular profit trim.

Choose a permitted no-dividend core campaign: q0>0, actual cost p0, support
f0<p0 remains fixed, no partial sale, price persistently above support, ACT on.
V(0)<=q0*(f0-p0)-paid/projected fees<0 regardless of market price growth.
Any legal NEW mobile lot starts with entry>its OWN floor by original preview,
so it adds negative floor-valued PnL after costs. Thus V(q)<V(0)<0 for ALL q>0,
even if the new CP1 floor is much higher than the old core entry price.
Unused initial authorization cannot refill, and the campaign never went flat.
No expansion is possible. Passive weight growth is not active risk escalation.

A feasible illustrative core100 at100, floor80 has roughly2000 downside and
can fit original .25% of1m core-risk sizing including fees. At price200 with
floor80 it has reached roughly2% passive weight (NAV also rises), but still cannot
finance a single add.
Original cash/industry/global space can all be ample. No shortage is needed.
Its frequency is unknown; we cannot label it a rare accepted user tradeoff.
It fails the stated capability to scale continuous winners without waiting
for a reversal. No whole historical coverage is needed to establish possibility.

INITIAL MOBILE DIFFERS
Mobile actually ratchets max(old line,I floor,fresh CP1 candidate) after old
line checks. CP1 new-frontier credit can emit receipts during continuous UP
without an E0 down/up pair. With bounded scale, candidates can rise above
cost, so a mobile trial can eventually finance a legal add. Slow floor, minlot,
fees and gaps can still delay beyond the main move; participation is not assured.
The current core-first reservation can choose the role that cannot earn such
credit. That is an algorithm/protection/allocation interaction, not a formula
implementation detail or permission to assign CP1 protection to old core.
