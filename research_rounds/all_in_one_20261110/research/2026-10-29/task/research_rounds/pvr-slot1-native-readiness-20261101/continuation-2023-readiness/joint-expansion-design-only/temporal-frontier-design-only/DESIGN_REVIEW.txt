Question and claim
Can distributed NEW price-frontier progress qualify fresh CP1 risk expansion,
while initial uncertainty stays in a shared, limited trial pool? The proposed
index measures distribution of positive progress, NOT independent samples,
probability, trend quality or guaranteed capital preservation. Real accepted
loss remains governed by actual protection and unchanged hard resources.

stock high or industry price. IMPORTANT: original receipt_consumed clears

h=5 is a NEW heuristic research threshold, despite familiar5/21 scale labels;

REJECT direct current-N gating: five a=.1 give N=5; adding a=1 changes it to
(1.5)^2/1.05=15/7, about2.14, despite a new strong positive frontier.
Select ONE explicit pre-coverage alternative: an attempt-local latch L.
After all original resets, L becomes true only at a completed observation

5. Matched manual counterexamples -- not executed indicator/unit tests
- Same ready scale and amount, same total log advance5s: one-day advance5s
  then flat gives one nonzero a and N=1; five advances of s on different days
  give five equal a and N=5. Both use the same endpoint. Five unequal advances
  generally give N<5; five effective days is not five literal up days.
- The .1,.1,.1,.1,.1,1 counterexample: a previously VALIDATED L survives the
  last large advance. If the first five had no original fresh CP1, they did NOT
  validate L; the strong day can still postpone first validation. Accepted
  concentration semantics, not monotonic trend-strength semantics. Jump+flat
  cannot validate; jump+micro-climb may eventually validate after the jump
  ages out or enough positive increments accumulate; qualification still needs
  a fresh CP1. No claim it is a strong trend.
- Falling highs/lows followed by a distributed six-day bounce can produce
  N>=5 and fresh CP1 while original far-line I remains active. After negative
  reset, the bounce can qualify BELOW the old high. T1 does NOT solve that
  countertrend failure; if exclusion of these bounces is mandatory, reject T1
  rather than adding another hidden direction gate. Real trial/sector risk
  absorbs accepted mistakes, with no hard cumulative-loss guarantee.
- A normal pullback without original negative/reset keeps F and validated L.
  Recovery below F contributes0; before first validation, missing new frontiers
  can delay it. After validation only fresh CP1 can create another risk request.
  A pullback large enough for original E0 negative clears proof; next recovery must earn it again. Existing core/mobile
  exits still act independently and can cause false exits/reentries.
- Failed attempt/new low then distributed new trend below former peak can
  requalify after original reset. Same initial prefix produces identical
  decisions before failure/winner branches separate; no hindsight reset.
- Monotonic core with unchanged far E0 floor can reach a validated latch and fresh CP1;
  it can expand if actual old/new risk and cash allow. No B-style deadlock.
  A valid minimum order may still be unaffordable, old trial transfer may not
  fit60%, or proof may mature only after the main move. No R1/ceiling increase.
- A proved campaign reset while holding20% cannot obtain a fresh2% trial;
  existing commitments remain. Unproved trial stocks across industries cannot
  fill60%: they must share10%/.5% until actually proved and legally migrated.

Verdict
One revised candidate T1-L is specified, with explicit stock-scoped migration and
industry-total-cap changes. It is NOT implementation-ready as a one-line gate,
not proved superior, and not permission to run coverage or financial paths.
Its falsifiable claim is distributed-vs-impulsive market permission usable
under finite trial capacity, not risk-free expansion or elimination of bearish
rebounds. Strongest unresolved semantic costs are resetting good pullbacks versus
allowing distributed bearish recoveries, and a validation latch preserving
possibly stale permission. Jump plus micro-climb can eventually validate
before or after the old impulse rolls away if N crosses on another fresh CP1; this is not a
complete one-impulse amplitude attribution or durable-mainline classifier; no new filter is selected here.
If root continues, only the unchanged-source matched manual logic and native
conservation/clock cases above should be designed/approved first. No new scale,
window, threshold grid, feature cache, indicator or account was calculated.
