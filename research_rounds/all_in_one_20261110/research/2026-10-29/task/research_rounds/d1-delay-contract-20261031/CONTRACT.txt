D1 proposed execution-pressure contract. Review/synthetic only; NOT activated.

Scope: all new authorized core and independent mobile first/add/reentry orders
in both B3 and B_diag. Signals, ranking, sizing and all risk/capital constraints
remain unchanged. This is a new bounded pressure execution policy, not a claim
that the old daily-credit delay1 contract already covered these roles.

Calendar and lease
1. Market day means the next session in the existing frozen market.sessions['A']
   source calendar (currently the union90 industry daily-date proxy), not a civil
   day and not independently certified exchange-calendar coverage.
2. Admit/fund an original order at its original opening, with the original
   canonical receipt consumed once. No increase of quantity, cash target or
   priority is granted by D1. It starts with the original one-session lease.
3. t is the day of the first distinct native FillWindow which would actually
   permit a positive fill after market/quantity/fees/cash and original resource
   tests. Deliberately skip this fill once, retain the SAME order, token, pool,
   structure/attempt and real reservations. Set earliest retry to session t+1
   and hard buy expiry to its close. A second event on t cannot bypass the wait.
4. No such eligible first window before original day-end => original expiry;
   no lease extension and no delay credit. No t+1 session in the frozen known
   calendar => unresolved at data boundary, not a fabricated next day/fill.
5. At t+1 use only the original execution window(s). Halt/no eligible window,
   missing data, no legal quantity or partial remainder => cancel at that
   close. Never roll a buy into t+2. This is at most one trading-day extension,
   not indefinite waiting for some later eligible window/GTC.

Validity and competing signals
6. At t close, and before retry using the last completed decision information,
   retain only when ACT permits added risk, original industry E0 episode AND
   attempt are still active, original stock I/E0 structure remains valid, its
   original protection has not failed, and no sell/order-identity conflict is
   present. Unknown required input cancels with an explicit unknown reason.
   Do NOT require another fresh CP1: this executes the existing authorization.
7. Pending protection is the original admitted line, adjusted only by already
   supported corporate-action unit transformations. Current code has no daily
   pending-line ratchet. Do not copy a later CP1/floor into it as new permission.
8. Keep original explicit cancel rules: original sell/ACT cancellation, industry
   invalidation, and the existing probe-order cancellation on stage upgrade
   take priority. The last is a valid same-attempt cancellation, not a failure
   of D1 or permission to migrate a still-open order invisibly. Log its reason.
9. Same-stock new signals while the order is pending cannot create another
   order, top up or reset t/deadline. They are observed, not consumed for this
   pending order and not replayed later. Cancellation does not refund the old
   receipt or award a credit. A later genuinely fresh signal can start a new
   order, which has its own one-window delay. Core epoch locks remain original.

Retry quantity, price and resources
10. At t+1 recompute legal quantity at that known opening price/fees/date, with
    original authorized remaining shares and remaining gross budget as upper
    bounds. Recheck actual cash/reserve, pool/sector/stock and nominal-risk
    constraints (including account3% for B3 only). Exclude this order's own
    reservation once when testing its replacement occupancy; include all other
    pending/old-attempt holdings. No expected sale proceeds may be spent.
11. Quantity may only shrink, never increase; corporate-action share transforms
    preserve economic bounds. Preserve the order ID, consumed receipt, partial
    fee accrual/minimum and original signal time. Amend the real native order
    and budget representation in one callback; release only the explicitly
    canceled remainder/fee reservation. Native cancellation is synchronous in
    this simulator. Do not create a second synthetic fill or second reservation.
12. The native ledger supports affordability/minimum/partial logic but the
    v3 callback asserts execution price <= old admitted mirror unit price.
    Therefore carry-and-call-fill unchanged is NOT implementable for an up-gap.
    A bounded reprice/readmission amendment is required before fill, retaining
    original quantity/gross limits and all caps. No such adapter is activated.
    Lack of a legal amended quantity cancels; do not silently bypass the check.
13. Existing reservations remain real occupancy while waiting. Revalidate/
    amend retained orders before funding new H1-ranked intents; execute them
    in the normal buy phase without another ranking/token admission. Any
    released remainder becomes available only after the actual amendment/
    cancel callback. Preserve original sale-first daily-proxy execution order.
14. A t+1 partial fill has already spent the wait. Matching granularity and
    per-order accumulated fees remain native; no repeated delay/minimum on
    each slice. Cancel the remaining buy at t+1 close; keep actual holdings.

Sell obligations
15. Sell orders also skip their first eligible positive window on t and cannot
    execute before t+1. Unlike buys, their original exit obligation remains
    latched after t+1 until legally completed; unavailable t+1 does not erase
    the exit. New/repeated exit reasons or price recovery do not reset delay or
    cancel the obligation. Partial remainder has no second wait. Real inventory
    and risk remain until fills; money only after native actual settlement.

Minimal implementation boundaries still requiring integration review
- Replace day-expiry/credit behavior for D1 orders only; disable core/old-ADD
  credit generation/consumption for these orders, with no changes to normal.
- Add explicit carried-order retry phase; present opening handles only new
  intents whose earliest_execution_at equals that opening.
- Add order-bound t/t+1/deadline, last-known validity and amendment audit fields
  to both native order metadata and budget mirror; serialize them with engine
  provenance. No field can be inferred retroactively from a later fresh signal.
- Do not weaken v3 stock/core/mobile/fee/NAV checks to make carried orders fill.

The accompanying state tests are a pure lifecycle specification, plus small
native-ledger fixtures/counterexamples. They do not mean a historical D1 runner
has passed or that any D1 performance was measured. Existing OHLCV/opening
liquidity and same-opening sell-cash-reuse limitations remain unchanged.
