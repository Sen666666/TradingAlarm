# M9: persistent stock failure, research-only frozen hypothesis

Frozen before new strategy outcomes. Baseline M7 mainline_m7_downturn_cash_batch_v6/engine.py SHA256 b81f78fc04dec3709191258230c2e2fa7580f8658315c78e9b25bf3fea318c2e; M8 is only the reviewed grouped-sell execution scaffold. No historical strategy run in this build. No parameter scan, allocation, sizing, funding, reentry, sector cap or shared trial-risk pool changes.

For the same active round r at completed market session t, let P(r,t) mean ALL of: sector state == 0, mainline M == 0, individual stock M3 state == 0, round net liquidation PnL <= 0. All state values must be known (0 or 1), and the close must be observed, price eligible, not source-invalid or synthetic, valid with positive finite raw_close, and in the same quote lineage as the round. Unknown or absent facts do not count.

Exact PnL formula is inherited net(a,t,'raw_close'): cashflow_r + dividend_r + quantity_r(t) * raw_close_t * (1-SLIP) - fee(stock,t,quantity_r(t),raw_close_t*(1-SLIP),'sell'). Cashflow includes all actual buy/sell trade cashflows and charged fees of that round; dividend follows inherited corporate-action accounting. This is neither account PnL nor unrealized lot PnL. Use the actual holdings and cashflow at each close. Never reconstruct yesterday from today's changed holdings.

Store each completed-close evidence record inside the existing round dictionary, already included in the atomic snapshot. A new EXPANSION retirement marker at t requires P(r,t) AND the saved P(r,prev(t)), whose saved day equals the immediately preceding canonical market session and whose round ID matches r. A missing, unknown, invalid, noncontiguous or different-round observation cannot confirm. Two closes is the minimal ex-ante persistence hypothesis, not a fitted delay. Record even false/unknown observations so a later true day cannot bridge them. Same-day repeated hook cannot substitute for a second session.

Hard exits remain first, unchanged: I invalid, I price protection and per-layer CP protection. INITIAL lots retain all original ordinary/trial exits. Soft marking occurs after inherited CLOSE protection decisions and before existing cash planning; it never replaces a hard exit. Mark only unprotected EXPANSION lots in unprotected accounts. Marker is irrevocable at later eligible OPEN, even after recovery. M8 grouped same-stock sell_floor, stable lot-ID quantity assignment, fees/slippage, T+1, legal odd-lot rules, INITIAL preservation, partial-lot markers and original cash planning are unchanged. No synthetic quote or new shares.

Exports include explicit reason PERSISTENT_STOCK_NONPROFIT_EXPANSION_RETIREMENT and frozen current/prior evidence on each marker and sale; daily evidence diagnostics in CANDIDATES; pending evidence in PENDING_RETIREMENTS. A separate ROUND_EXIT_EVIDENCE export exposes the final saved observation per round. Evidence and markers roll back atomically with the round; candidate logs roll back with inherited log lengths. Economic preflight remains fail closed.

Limitations: requiring whole-round nonpositive PnL can give back substantial prior profit. Two closes can lag abrupt regime breaks. Profitable rounds are NOT exempt from unchanged I/CP hard protection, but those floors may not lock all profits. This single hypothesis does not solve fat-tail holding or profit protection and must not be represented as a sufficient strategy. Historical and actual-data examples must be distinguished from M9 replay, whose changed holdings/cashflows can change net PnL. No CONTRACT_APPROVAL is created here.
