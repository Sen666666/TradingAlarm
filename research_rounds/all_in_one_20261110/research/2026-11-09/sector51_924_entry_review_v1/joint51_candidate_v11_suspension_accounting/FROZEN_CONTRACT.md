## Sizing and chronology
SEED is exactly the minimum dated legal buy quantity. Its all-in cost cannot exceed 2% of account NAV frozen at the attempt's signal CLOSE. This is only an extreme-price minimum-unit ceiling. ADD all-in budget is min(2% attempt frozen NAV, max(0,4% attempt frozen NAV - existing attempt shares times current raw price)). No risk-based share resizing.
Buy-only capital limits retain 10% per stock, 10% independently for each sector, and 10% account cash reserve. Fees count toward conservative capital reservation. Price appreciation over limits does not force sales. These are explicit baseline capital ceilings, not optimized priorities. Signal CLOSE plans a maximum legal quantity from then-known data/budget; next common OPEN can reduce but cannot increase that quantity. Entry gaps use actual raw OPEN, no current H/L/close/volume.
At each OPEN: validate source and corporate-action bridge; execute all locked exits/known protection breaches first; then form all ADD requests, then all SEED requests. Freeze post-exit OPEN account NAV and pre-buy exposure, allocate reservations sequentially without fills changing ranking, then apply fills. ADD order key is unconsumed CP timestamp then symbol; SEED key is A/B signal timestamp then symbol. Log rejected quantities/capital conflicts and identify this ordering as transparent baseline, not demonstrated selection advantage. T+1 is enforced per lot. Full exit preserves residual same-day unsellable lots as locked exits. Raw OPEN times 1.0005 buys and times .9995 sells; exact dated fee components. Conditional proxy, not execution certification.

## ADD evidence and protections
Sector strengthening requires n >= N/2 at both current and immediately previous CLOSE, plus current same-member three-day net diffusion >0. Entire current attempt's hypothetical liquidation, including all past trade cashflows, recognized NET80 entitlement, and all remaining sell fees, must be strictly positive at plan CLOSE and executable OPEN. No future prices. A CP must be produced after actual seed OPEN (same day's CLOSE qualifies), receipt=True, candidate finite positive, unchanged lineage (source_kind, source_path, segment_id), not subsequently breached by CLOSE/OPEN, and never actually consumed. Identity includes symbol, receipt date, candidate value and source identity. Keep only latest valid unconsumed CP; new receipt supersedes an older unconsumed one. Only an actual ADD fill consumes CP. Failed orders do not consume it, do not generate a new CP, and may retry on a later qualified CLOSE while it remains valid.
I protection is nondecreasing in signal coordinates within an attempt. Every ADD lot has its own nondecreasing CP/I floor, ratcheted using later valid CP receipts. For seed core, known I invalid or raw OPEN/CLOSE at/below carried I protection locks full exit; for ADD lots, raw OPEN/CLOSE at/below each carried CP/I floor locks that layer. At a valid CLOSE, test existing floors, then ratchet to valid current I and CP and test the resulting floor before making new ADD plans. A breached CP is invalidated before replacement. Corporate-action factor translation may reduce the raw numeric floor but never lowers its economic/signal-coordinate protection.
Unconfirmed trial (zero historical actual ADD fills) exits at next common OPEN following sector FALSE or I invalid CLOSE. Sector UNKNOWN is recorded, blocks ADD, and does not itself sell. Once any ADD actually filled, seed core is mature and does not exit merely because sector becomes FALSE. Existing locked exit always overrides ADD. Unknown I/CP is recorded and blocks fresh ADD, never converted to FALSE. Invalid held source/price or unadmitted factor/equity bridge stops certified account path rather than silently marking stale inventory.
