Research-only build authorized; historical execution is NOT authorized. M7 cash v6 is the sole baseline, SHA256 b81f78fc04dec3709191258230c2e2fa7580f8658315c78e9b25bf3fea318c2e. ## Frozen single hypothesis
An INITIAL lot may adopt a new structural stop only at a completed valid CLOSE after that lot has actually been bought (same-day OPEN fill then CLOSE receipt allowed). The receipt must be created today by the existing engine, in the same round and quote lineage, with finite positive candidate F and adjusted CLOSE strictly greater than F, unconsumed and not invalid at adoption. No historical CP lookup, delayed adoption of an old receipt, or recovery of superseded/invalidated tickets.

Using actual current round holdings and cashflows, A is the contemporaneous admitted adjustment factor in the round, Q is current whole-round quantity, pCP=(F/A)*(1-SLIP). net_at_CP = round.cashflow + round.dividend + Q*pCP - fee(stock,day,Q,pCP,'sell'). net_close is exact inherited net(a,day,'raw_close'). Both must be strictly positive. Zero fails. These are round metrics, not unrealized profit or account profit. They include inherited realized transactions, fees and dividends. Adoption requires no pre-existing account/lot exit. Later lot quantity changes do not retrospectively change stored evidence.

The INITIAL lot stores its own seed_cp_floor in the existing adjusted signal-price coordinate, with source CP ID, source date, adoption date, origin and immutable evidence. Adopted floor only rises: max(previous adopted floor, eligible new F). Actual protection is max(account ifloor, adopted floor). A lower/equal new receipt does not weaken or overwrite the recorded stronger protection. Every new adoption/raise again requires both positive-net tests. Existing protection is not removed when profit subsequently falls, CP is consumed, or a later CP supersedes the buy ticket. This is a separately established price constraint, not revival of the old ticket. Unadopted old receipts remain unavailable forever. New rounds never inherit old stops.

Price is actual raw_open*(1-SLIP), NOT CP price. CP consumed/invalid flags are not price-breach signals: baseline invalidate_cp also invalidates consumed buy tickets. ## Required evidence and metric limits
Every INITIAL CLOSE emits admission/raise/rejection evidence: current net, candidate CP net when computable, source/receipt identity, old/new stop, raw and adjusted close, admitted factor, round cashflow/dividend and all then-current lot quantities/floors. Emit actual layered-floor pressure before and after adoption. For this diagnostic only, each surviving lot is hypothetically liquidated at its actual effective floor (ADD: its floor; INITIAL: max(I, adopted floor); EXPANSION-SEED: I), converted through current A, with slippage and a separate sell fee PER LOT. Thus the metric explicitly models separated lot exits/minimum commissions, not all shares sold at one CP or one optimistic commission. It is a static stress convention, not forecast trading dates/prices or actual executable guarantee. Exports show constituent floor/quantity/price/fee/proceeds, cashflow/dividend, and total. Zero quantity is omitted and never charged a liquidation fee.

net_at_CP hypothetically values ALL whole-round remaining shares at the candidate CP, whereas only INITIAL protection changes. Therefore positive net_at_CP does not guarantee positive actual layered pressure, nor positive realized profit. ADD inventory/realized cashflows/dividends can change eligibility. OPEN gaps, finite observation cadence and normal trend pullbacks can cause loss or early exit. This is structural protection with a positive-net admission gate, not guaranteed profit locking. Existing numeric factor changes are handled in the inherited signal coordinate; source/lineage must remain admitted. No use of CP_EVENTS INVALIDATED alone as a structural signal.

All state lives in existing atomic-snapshotted lots/rounds; diagnostics in candidates roll back with existing logs. History entrypoint is disabled. Independent review, source hashes and synthetic QA are required before a separately authorized one-time historical comparison to M7.
