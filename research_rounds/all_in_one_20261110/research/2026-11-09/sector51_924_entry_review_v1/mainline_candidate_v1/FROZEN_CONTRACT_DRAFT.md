# M mainline allocation candidate, draft 1
Research only; no trading or production edits. No historical M run before explicit contract approval and synthetic PASS. One hypothesis, not tuned to outcomes. Baseline is joint51_candidate_v9_facts, exact same facts, raw OPEN +/-5bp cost proxy, Decimal fee/cash, fixed current 1692 members / 50 sectors (not PIT); 17 unclassified excluded. Original A/B admission retained, so UNKNOWN->TRUE and existing TRUE stocks without A/B can be missed. All episode labels are evaluation only.

## Mainline evidence
Compute strict per-stock complete common-day trailing 5/10/20 returns exactly as sector_strength_evidence_v1/build_strength.py; within-sector medians minus all1692-valid-window median, times100, are RS in percentage points. Missing members do not enter median, fixed N remains breadth and breakout denominator, K/U always disclosed. Half/breakout predicates use lower/upper counts: known count reaching N/2 is TRUE, known+unknown still below N/2 is FALSE, otherwise UNKNOWN. Undefined required statistics make that branch UNKNOWN, never zero-filled. Q requires current sector M3 TRUE and known n/N>=1/2 at current and previous common CLOSE. S=Q and RS10>0 and RS20>0 and absolute return10 median>0. F=Q and RS5>0 and RS5>previous common CLOSE RS5 and absolute return5 median>0 and breakout20_count*2>=N. M=S OR F under three-valued logic. UNKNOWN blocks new M demand and cannot itself cause sale. diff=0 does not veto Q/S/F. E=max(RS5,0)*n/N; M must also have finite E to queue. W=(RS5<0 AND RS10<0 at two consecutive common CLOSEs) OR (sector M3 FALSE at two consecutive common CLOSEs). UNKNOWN breaks corresponding streak.

## Queue and capital
All legally planned M requests precede all non-M SEED requests. Group M requests by industry; industry key=(-E,-consecutive_M_TRUE_days, minimum(original CP date for ADD / original A/B date for SEED, sid) among its requests, sector name). Inside one industry all ADD first then SEED, each by original CP / original seed date and sid. This makes intra-industry ADD priority transitive while maintaining deterministic sector ties. Non-M seeds follow original A/B date/sid. No fixed count, equal industry budget, shared risk pool or mechanical industry cap. Per-stock10%, cash floor10%, original attempt NAV single2% / round4% slots and original20-positive-volume capacity remain. Price growth above a buy cap does not itself sell.
SEED is once-only dated legal minimum. CLOSE and OPEN ADD quantity independently <= actual then-current round shares, floor to dated legal buy quantity; OPEN also <= CLOSE plan and capacity. Positive net liquidation is not risk coverage. Each filled ADD reports whole-round pre-add net profit and incremental hypothetical liquidation loss at known I/CP floor, including both buy and sell fees and sell slippage; no stop fill guarantee.

## Independent information confirmation
At CLOSE when M TRUE, held source/price/I/CP receipt known, current I target TRUE, unconsumed valid CP, no protected/locked exits, and hypothetical whole-round net liquidation>0, log INFORMATION_CLOSE_PASS, regardless of capacity/slot/cash. Store proof id, close date/net and CP id as pending confirmation; this is provisional evidence, not actual ADD and not historical maturity. At next OPEN after mandatory protection exits, recheck same CP id, valid unconsumed CP, raw OPEN above I and CP, no locked exits, net liquidation>0. Pass sets historical information_confirmed=True for this round even if ADD gets zero funding or cannot form a legal quantity. Failure logs WITHDRAWN and clears provisional proof, leaving any earlier historical confirmation intact. A historical confirmation is monotone for this round and never implies consumed CP. Filling ADD consumes CP once and independently records actual ADD count.
Original sector FALSE trial exit is preserved with historical information confirmation replacing actual ADD as the maturity test: NOT confirmed_history AND sector FALSE locks whole-round next-OPEN exit. A provisional CLOSE proof cannot exempt that CLOSE exit. Mandatory exits and T+1 locks occur before OPEN information review; review cannot unlock or resurrect a round. I/CP protection exits are unchanged. Capacity/legal size are execution gates, separate from information evidence.

## Directed transfer, no future target coverage
At signal CLOSE after all protection/CP/state updates, ticket refresh and payable cash-dividend transfer, form requests for next OPEN. Freeze a donor plan only when there is nonzero legal M demand AND a cash shortage. Determine close desired M cost in queue order under plan qty/capacity, attempt/round and per-stock caps, ignoring only cash. No non-M demand enters shortage. Start with actual close cash; credit only legally sellable mandatory protected exits already frozen by this CLOSE, valued at close*(1-slip) minus exact fee. Freeze planning NAV at close, cash floor0.10*close NAV. Only when desired M cost>0, shortage=max(0, desired M all-in cost + cash floor - simulated cash). With zero M demand no transfer occurs even if cash is below its floor. Current CLOSE prices are estimates, not promised fills.
Donor priority: (1) round with zero historical ADD, not historical/provisional information confirmed, sector M explicitly FALSE, both industry and stock M3 known and E finite, no mandatory exit; sell its minimum SEED core whole. Sort individual FALSE first, then E ascending, original seed date, sid. This is giving way to a stronger opportunity, not necessarily a fallen stock; later emerging mainlines can be missed and turnover costs incurred. (2) W TRUE industries, only ADD layers, never SEED core, oldest layer buy day then sid/lot id. A current M TRUE industry is never a donor (including conflicting W due to slow/fast timing). Donor plan contains concrete round/lot ids, maximum quantity and signal evidence. Next OPEN T+1 is checked; close plan quantity must be legally sellable on that next common day. Selling order quantities are dated sell legal; to cover shortage choose smallest legal share amount whose estimated net proceeds >= remaining shortage, clipped at eligible shares, allowing only unavoidable lot rounding. Fees may require one extra lot. Whole seed lots can overshoot shortage. No sale for UNKNOWN or diff=0 alone. No hypothetical future M or desire to cover more industries.
At next OPEN: preflight all held, actual pending and retained/paused tickets, accrue reviewed entitlements, perform original I/CP/locked exits, then resolve provisional information confirmations. Freeze allocation NAV and exposures after mandatory exits, before directed transfers; use this same NAV for stock/cash caps for rest of OPEN (conservative, fees do not lower reserve). Re-evaluate noncash legal size of surviving M requests at OPEN, never beyond CLOSE plan or current holding cap. For surviving desired M cost>0, actual shortage=max(0, desired cost + fixed0.10*NAV - current cash); otherwise zero. The stock cap uses this fixed pre-transfer OPEN NAV, not a strict post-fee instantaneous weight limit; actual post-trade concentration is reported. Iterate frozen donor list only; remove exited/unavailable/T+1-locked lots, reduce max to actual shares, and sell no more than frozen planned quantity. Recalculate remaining shortage after actual net proceeds; OPEN cannot add donor lots, increase frozen quantity or replace vanished receiver requests. Transfers execute before allocation and money can be used only after actual net sale cash is credited. Then allocate all requests in frozen evidence priority using actual available cash; CP consumes on fills only. No circular optimization or receiver funding assumptions.
Log planned gap and donor quantities, OPEN survivor demand/gap, every cancelled/shrunk donor, actual donor cash, receiver requested/filled cash and residual cash. Gaps can leave sold cash idle because small requests fail under legal lots or other caps; record without rewriting prior CLOSE plans. Original protection priority always dominates both donor and receiver.
