# ACT-only continuous-account analysis: frozen definitions

Only read completed native ledger outputs. Never instantiate an account or runner, change data/model, or rerun economics. Initial CNY 1,200,000, empty holdings, 2022-01-01 through 2026-05-15. Baseline PVR and NOACT use the same 1,709-stock / 90-industry frozen universe. Removing ACT is the sole treatment; ordinary qualification, entry, ADD, reduction, cash and pool limits remain.

- Annual NAV return uses prior-year final NAV (initial cash for 2022), including this starting NAV in annual running-peak MDD. Full-period return and MDD include initial cash. 2026 is partial through May 15, not annualized.
- Complete stock rounds are strict 0-to-0 holding spells, with every overlapping buy layer, ADD, partial sale, split and accrued dividend included. A complete exit and reentry at the same timestamp starts a new round. Layer intervals are independently checked against the actual trade/split replay. Open rounds remain open and marked, never force-liquidated.
- Round PnL is all layer trade cash + booked dividend accrual + remaining inventory mark. Booked corporate actions stay in the model's account; corporate-action terms being historically available and actual external payout are UNKNOWN, a separate limitation, not a sample exclusion.
- Mean gain/loss and payoff are cash-PnL statistics on complete rounds. Round return denominator is cumulative actual BUY gross plus BUY fees across every layer, not initial-entry capital, peak cash occupied, or NAV. Tail losses use complete round PnL and this cumulative-capital return separately.
- Closing-year grouping assigns the entire completed multi-year round PnL to its exit year and is not annual NAV PnL. Birth-year grouping anchors a round to its first chronological layer's immutable stock structure birth; multi-birth rounds are explicitly marked. Initial layers are separately labelled, never substituted for full rounds.
- Across-account matching never uses account round/layer sequence numbers. Stock + stock_structure birth is used for signal-lineage attribution. Multiple rounds/layers sharing a birth are aggregated. This is a signal-episode accounting bridge, not an invented paired trade. Birth UNKNOWN remains separate if a verified native structure is absent.
- Frozen original-I labels identify the same birth events for both arms. big60 = complete60 and MFE60 >=30% and return60 >=20%, post-hoc only. Early participation is a positive actual proxy-ledger close on at least one of the first six common sessions from label entry_day, including preexisting holdings. Early weight is the six-day average of raw marked position/NAV including zeroes. Added/lost are event participation changes. Recovered-by-removing-pause means NOACT-only early participation with baseline ACT off on at least one of those dates; it is not proof of an isolated direct ACT effect.
- Exposure is raw marked inventory/NAV, plus number of stocks and largest single-stock NAV fraction. It is nominal market exposure, not a bound on loss. Total buy principal is turnover, not incremental capital injected. Fees and 5bp fill slippage are already in NAV.
- Report actual ledger share counts (shares, not lots), dividends as accrued versus spendable, and actual buy/sell rows for 南网科技 688248.SH and the 2024-09-24/2026 early windows. Historical exchange tradability remains UNKNOWN under the native open proxy.
- No OOS, PIT, causal-execution or live-trading suitability claim. Fixed future pool, preknown corporate-action terms and open-proxy assumptions remain.
