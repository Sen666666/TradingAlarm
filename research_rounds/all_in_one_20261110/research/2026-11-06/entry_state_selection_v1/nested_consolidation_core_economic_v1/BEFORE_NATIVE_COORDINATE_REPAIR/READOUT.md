# Fixed 22-stock nested-consolidation core probe

## Finding

The fixed nested10/10 plus prior-amount contraction rule does generate genuinely earlier local entries, including winners. It also adds failed probes. In this bounded descriptive experiment, adding LOCAL to PRICE did not improve the completed, comparable paths. This is not an account test, unseen OOS, a general strategy ranking, or evidence to remove the original births.

## Interpretation and boundary

The additional setup is not merely a new PVR score or a gate on existing births. It can enter before20High, but its benefits are sparse and can be outweighed by extra failures. It changes initial floor and risk-sized quantity as well as entry timing. A pure constant-quantity timing effect was not tested. Existing TRE-1 has a10-day box too, but uses E0/G, ATR/distance/movement/down-day gates and current volume confirmation; the present independent nested10/10 and prior-volume contraction is different while sharing consolidation-breakout ancestry.

No shared-account/full-system promotion is supported. The next valid decision is whether this bounded evidence justifies further mechanism work, not whether these retrospective totals warrant deployment.
