## Exactly one candidate and objective
Age groups are fixed: 1–5,6–20,21–60,61–120,121+. For each group g, N_g is the sum of observed risk days. Every currently mature ordinary terminal observation j supplies q_gj=1/N_g and its uncut exact Y_j. Duplicate Y values may merge. qU_g=U_g/N_g, qM_g=M_g/N_g, q0_g=1−sum_j q_gj−qU_g−qM_g. No smoothing, resampling or fitted baseline hyperparameters.

eta_i=beta·z_i, five coefficients, no intercept. Z_g(eta)=q0+qU+qM+sum_j q_gj exp(eta Y_j). h0=q0/Z, hU=qU/Z, hM=qM/Z, h_g(dY)=q_g(dY)exp(etaY)/Z. U/M/continuation have zero sufficient statistic only; they are NOT assigned economic Y=0. This imposes fixed hU:hM:h0 ratios within an age group; no independent feature model of missingness. Unknown may be informative and no bias correction is claimed.

NLL(beta)=sum_i,g n_ig log Z_g(beta·z_i)−sum_{known ordinary i}(beta·z_i)Y_i+0.5 beta' diag(4,4,4,16,16) beta, with constants omitted. Gradient=sum_i,g n_ig mu_g(eta_i)z_i−sum_known Y_i z_i+Lambda beta. Hessian=sum_i,g n_ig Var_g(T|eta_i)z_i z_i'+Lambda, where T=Y for ordinary atoms and T=0 for the other observation categories. Therefore strictly convex, unique finite ridge optimum with finite inputs. All events including U/M use terminal-day logZ. beta=0 is the fixed no-feature baseline.
