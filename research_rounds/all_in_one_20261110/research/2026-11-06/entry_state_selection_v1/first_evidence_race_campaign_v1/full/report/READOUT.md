# Local initial-layer campaign comparison

Four independent-stock conditional arms share the original initial permits. A holds original I; B exits if DOWN wins and stays cash; C retries original A/B births; D retries original native FAST CORE/flat fresh CP, retaining actual-fill consumption and CP role protection. No additional layer and no shared account. D was added after the fixed 33-permit ABC technical sample and before the full run.

All trading ends with the same original I root. The experiment does not test continuous following across later I roots. Selling before a later rally or buying back at a higher price is not a verdict; use complete campaign net, expectancy, payoff, coverage, capital-days and drawdown.

PAIRWISE_COMMON_KNOWN.csv gives A-B, B-C, B-D, A-C and A-D on their own common-known samples. FIRST_ATTEMPT_AND_CAMPAIGN.csv separates first attempt and full campaign on each arm's known and four-arm common-known sets. ALL_PERMIT_COVERAGE.csv retains no-fill, unknown, censored and unpaid rights.

Capital-days mean purchase cash including fee held at each common-day close, not market-value exposure. Drawdown is local cash plus conditional net liquidation plus recognized receivable, and is only a known-prefix statistic where marks fail. It is not portfolio/account drawdown. Receivables are not spendable; closed accrual outcomes may still have unpaid claims. Conditional C/D recovery omits cross-stock top-two allocation and shared cash and may overstate executable recovery availability.
