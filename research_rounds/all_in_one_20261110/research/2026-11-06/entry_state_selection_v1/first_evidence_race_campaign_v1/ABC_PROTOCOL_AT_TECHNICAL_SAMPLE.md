## Cash, sizing and claims

Initial principal for every arm is its actual initial buy gross + independent fee. Initial unused 24,000 allowance is not C recovery capital. C liquid cash is actual net sale proceeds plus NET80 dividends actually paid, less subsequent actual purchases and fees. Receivables are separately valued and never spendable. Original pay_day is CLOSE; payout first becomes usable next common OPEN. Available sale proceeds are usable only after actual sell execution; no simultaneous sell/rebuy OPEN. Recovery budget=min(24,000, available_cash), riskmax=3,000, original dated lot rule and prior20 capacity retained. Recompute at OPEN, decrement integer legal lots including fees until cash and risk pass. No forced minimum lot, leverage, future dividend advance or future add funding.

Maintain per-attempt cashflow and campaign spendable cash separately, because native entry_open resets the attempt cash to negative buy cost. Claim identity includes window, arm, campaign and canonical economic_event_id, with owner attempt and actual record-close quantity. An event can have only one record owner within a campaign; subsequent reentry cannot steal an earlier claim. Cash payout to an exited attempt must credit its campaign wallet. Source event ID is not redefined by corrected date or evidence version.

Only original admitted entitlement evidence is allowed. No new factual cash source, factor-implied gift shares or tax claim. NET80 is a scenario, not actual tax. Pure-cash coordinate bridge must retain original pending/held checks; stock dividends/splits/rights not supported by admitted evidence remain UNKNOWN. Each arm's actual held/record intervals, including exited-but-entitled claims, receive independent coverage adjudication. B can be known after selling before A's later unknown action; do not copy A's financial label. Conversely a sold position can still own an unresolved entitlement and cannot be washed clean.
