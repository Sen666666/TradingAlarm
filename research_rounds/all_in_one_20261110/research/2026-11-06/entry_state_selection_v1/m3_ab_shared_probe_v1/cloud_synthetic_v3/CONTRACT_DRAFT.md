The shared-account service consumes already-established intentions, valuation/risk charges, eligibility evidence and equity events. It must not reimplement M3, the original financial-event formulas, NET80, CP identification, or PVR ranking. The original lifecycle owns quantity, cost, line, core/add registration, and per-cycle income. The wallet owns shared cash, receivables/claims, reservations, capital and risk obligations, and auditable pool labels. An original UNKNOWN state may discard an active strategy state but must not discard archived inventory or claims from wallet accounting.

## 3. Daily clock and queue

1. CLOSE: original AB checks eligibility and produces intentions. Original PVR selects at most two initial industry opportunities, ranking PVR descending then sid within industry. SELECTED_PENDING is an opportunity slot, not a broker order and not a cash reservation. Failed OPEN opportunities do not refill from the industry's third-ranked stock.
2. Known sells and confirmed effective equity settlements occur before OPEN admission allocation. A pay-date CLOSE transfer becomes available at the next OPEN, never at that same day's earlier OPEN. The caller supplies event effective-open identity and exact cash/claim amount; the wallet does not calculate ex/pay dates or corporate-action entitlements.
3. Obtain one externally certified current N/valuation snapshot after those events. Freeze promotion eligibility for stocks already held before new queue fills. Apply only stock-specific grants derived from original AB full-cycle NET80: this cycle's cash, recognized receivables and remaining inventory net realization; original CLOSE and OPEN marks both positive, fresh CP after executed first probe, M3 TRUE. No other stock's profit, previous-cycle claim, sector-wide transfer, or same-OPEN freshly filled position qualifies.
4. Require a caller-supplied immutable all-market order sequence with a named frozen policy. Original within-industry ranking alone does not decide cross-industry allocation under scarcity. Never sort sectors, dictionary keys or sids to silently create that policy. The caller/root must freeze it before real data integration.
5. Process that exact sequence once. Each accepted OPEN reserve creates a real modeled obligation; live and cancel_pending cash remain unavailable. Full/partial fills transfer those obligations to holdings and debit only the filled cash. Cancel request alone releases nothing; confirmed cancel releases the unfilled reservation. A rejection consumes the opportunity, without queue insertion, replacement or capital pre-reservation at CLOSE.

## 4. Promotion, dividends and UNKNOWN

Pool transfer is purely a ledger-label move for one stock's current cycle, preserving cash, quantities, costs, protective lines and claims. The service does not calculate profitability, but checks the presence of stock/cycle-specific evidence flags and references from the AB adapter. Both initial and ADD layers are included, with fees recognized exactly once and dividends tied only to that cycle's registered core/add inventory. Ex creates a receivable, not spendable cash. Payment transfers the recognized claim to cash on the audited clock. Old-cycle claims remain attached to their old cycle and cannot subsidize new-cycle promotion evidence.

UNKNOWN holds/claims remain recorded at their last supplied value with explicit uncertainty; never turn them into zero-valued released capital. The conservative engineering boundary blocks new reservations and promotion if any held asset is UNKNOWN or N is uncertified. Known protective exits and already-confirmed claim settlement may continue before admission. This is a data-evidence safety freeze, not ACT cooling and not a strategy modification demonstrated on original data. Existing live/cancel_pending orders still occupy capital, and factual fills/cancellations must still be reconciled. Last-known marks do not certify NAV. The source adapter must repair and reconcile the evidence gap before admissions resume.
