1. Remaining economic acquisition cost
The authoritative native live-layer dictionaries do not contain remaining basis.
They contain current qty and cumulative trading cash. Cumulative x.cash must not
be reused as remaining acquisition cost; x.initial_qty is not split-adjusted.
Minimal admission observer, driven only by actual native callbacks:
  actual fill: B_i = actual gross_cny + actual buy fees_cny.
  actual sale part: B_after = B_before * (q_before - q_sold) / q_before.
  stock split/bonus: monetary B is retained in a surviving original layer;
    qty/entry units follow the authoritative native callback, not a new CA model.
  layer made zero solely by native fractional-floor conversion: B goes zero and
    its disposal is recorded as nontrade admission-basis writeoff, never a sale.
This last surviving-layer treatment is a conservative admission-cost convention,
not a claim that the original native ledger had a tax-basis/fraction cost engine.
It must be frozen before outcomes and tested on integer and fractional cases.
Cash dividends do not reduce B. All native cash/quantity/NAV stay authoritative.

E_i is separately the actual gross execution price per share, excluding buy fees.
It follows native unit_cost split adjustment. It is not B_i/q_i after costs or
fractional writeoffs. F>=E therefore is not a fee-inclusive breakeven guarantee.

2. Eligibility for release of only this extra admission occupancy
At the same original clock and same known current units:
  protected_i = known AND F_i >= E_i AND native_mark_i > F_i
                AND no original exit responsibility AND protection still valid.
Core uses last decision-known last_core_line; mobile uses current live floor;
the original facts.factor converts the adjusted line into current raw units.
OPEN must not load today's later CLOSE protection. First hit is not permanent.
Unknown, breached line, or exit responsibility remains charged its actual B_i.
All original pool/stock/sector/cash/complete mark-floor constraints stay in force.

3. Numerator and pending transformations
  U = sum B_i over unprotected live new layers;
  P = sum(remaining_budget_cny + fee_reserved_cny) over open/cancel-pending buys.
Every unfilled share is charged; no protection inference is allowed pre-fill.
Pending-to-fill transformation charges actual gross+actual fee to the new layer
and removes precisely that gross+fee from the remaining order reservation. A
partial fill must not retain both the old full reservation and the new basis.
Cancellation request alone does not release P; actual native ACK does.
NR20 uses all live-layer B rather than only unprotected B.

4. Equity and atomic preview
Actual native equity is N, including its existing AR/payables and raw marks.
Reservation itself transfers available cash and does not expense a fee.
However a cap checked only against pre-fee N can reject its own fill at the edge.
For original CNY pricing exec=raw*(1+s), where s is the existing native slip:
  actual full-fill equity drag = fee + q*(exec - raw).
For an existing reserved order, full known fee+protocol-slip buffer is bounded
by fee_reserved + remaining_gross*s/(1+s), subject to the native price<=limit
contract. This is a conservative admission forecast, never a booked NAV debit.
A consistent reservation preview may use:
  N_guard = N - sum(existing pending fee+slip buffers)
              - new fee(q) - q*(new exec - new raw mark),
  U + P + new gross(q) + new fee(q) <= alpha * N_guard.
The new fee appears once; existing pending fees appear once. The native minimum
commission and its per-order cumulative state determine the fee, not q scaling.
For actual fill the producer must re-evaluate exact native fee/order state and
actual window.raw_open/execution_price before mutation and check projected
post-fill U+P and actual post-fill N. Same guarantee should hold in a post-check.
Market moves or floor/status regressions can make old U/N exceed alpha without
a trade: do not force sell; forbid additional buy, preserve exit/cancel contracts.
Known fee/slip projection is not protection against future gaps or market moves.
