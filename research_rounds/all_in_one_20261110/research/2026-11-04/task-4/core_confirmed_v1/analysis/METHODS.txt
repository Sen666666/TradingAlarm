Confirmed CORE fallback: prepared saved-book analysis only

Inputs
OFF: new_price_trend_v1/funding/runs/{long_OFF,strong_OFF}.
ON:  core_confirmed_v1/funding/runs/{long_ON,strong_ON}.
Long is one fresh1m continuous2022-2024 path; strong is fresh1m Jul2025-Jun2026.
No annual reseed, no importing OFF account state into ON. The producer/root runs
accounts. This directory only reads completed saved books after root notification.

Run policy
--prepare records code/input identities and expected outputs, without importing
the financial reader or running account analysis. --execute requires all four
DONE records and native B/daily/balances streams. Root invokes it only after ON
completion. No Account, RiskBook, strategy Runner, order, new indicator, complete
market preparation or data refresh is constructed by this analysis.

Authoritative financial bridge
Reuse unchanged new_price_trend_v1/analyze_saved_accounts.py::analyze and its
native_mark_reader.price. Redirect its OUT to this directory; old files remain
read-only. Mark means original FrozenMarket.latest_mark, including stale
theoretical ex-action references. Never replace it with facts.raw_close.
Actual trade consideration/fees plus once-recognized dividends plus actual
saved shares times native raw mark must equal every saved NAV. Native quantity,
cash, pool and NAV certificates remain checked. No residual plugs or deletion-PnL.

Periods and attribution
Full windows, own2022/2023/2024, Sep24-Dec2024, Sep24-Oct8 rally, Oct9-Dec2024,
Jul-Dec2025 and Jan-Jun2026. Each period uses its own preceding actual NAV;
global full-window DD never resets annually. DD/water are close-based. Turnover
is actual buy+sell gross divided by period mean NAV, not mechanical annualization.
Every period has all90 industries, including zero exposure. Semiconductor means
the fixed metadata industry group of all126 stocks, never the25 previously bought
layers. H1 wealth includes Dec31 carry stock; new buy cost and actual fills are
reported separately. H1 stock/industry/layer sums bridge actual period NAV change.

Risk and cash
Saved actual quantity and current last_core_line (CORE) or native mobile floor
are converted by the original facts.factor to raw units; native marks give held
nominal mark-to-line drawdown. Unknown lines remain unknown; a crossed line is
not called risk-free. Budget probe/confirmed risk includes original view/pending
responsibilities and is reported separately from independently read held risk.
Report total, probe and confirmed actual risk, cash availability/receivables,
order reserves and ACT changes. Retain a sparse active-industry daily panel of
held capital, current-line risk and probe/confirmed risk. Daily probe0.5% and
industry1% reference exceedances use actual CLOSE NAV/current lines, not entry
limits or initial quantity alone. They exclude pending obligations and are not
asserted to prove a request-time contract failure; the exact request-time gates
remain in the literal mechanism stream. Missing current line/factor stays
unknown and does not become zero. New confirmed CORE does not face probe0.5%/2%
constraints; industry1%, original I0.25% entry sizing and cash10% remain separate
contracts. Do not claim total risk is unchanged or that an initial size cap is a
guaranteed loss bound. Known pending-risk decomposition gaps are not plugged.

Mechanism identity and trajectories
Direct new confirmed CORE entry is real_buy_fill role=core AND pool=confirmed
in the immutable journal, not daily layer.pool (old CORE may migrate pools).
Use budget_order/layer links to entry, actual shares, I line, exits, net lifecycle
contribution and later same-stock actual buys after exit. core-confirmed.jsonl.gz
is an evidence/attempt stream; retain raw rows and link only explicit identities.
Check actual entry CP1_id against the successful attempt receipt_id, retaining
any missing/nonmatching links rather than inventing a successful association.
Unknown schema is declared rather than guessed. Native journal fills remain
the source of truth for actual mechanism adoption.

Opportunity and incumbent effects
Match actual filled cohorts by sid+normalized signal timestamp+role+original
source/I epoch(+canonical CP receipt for MOBILE), never cross-policy layer IDs.
Show common/lost/new actual cohorts and changed old MOBILE events, including
new-path same-signal confirmed CORE, current holdings/barriers and literal native
OFF/ON funding outcomes. Common cohorts with different size, net or entry pool
remain in the change context. These are actual path differences, not proof every change was
directly caused by one refusal. Old positive winners/top10 are evaluation cohorts
only. Report overlap of original holding windows, capital weights, first entries
and actual stock contribution. Do not use future returns in the live rule.

Conservation and limitations
Sum all-stock and all-event contribution deltas exactly equals ON-OFF terminal
NAV difference for each whole window. Period layer/stock/industry wealth deltas
equal differences in actual period wealth, not return percentages on a common
invented H1 principal. New CORE cohort profit alone is not strategy increment:
other CORE/MOBILE/industry/ACT paths and opportunity costs remain in the bridge.
Current survivor/industry snapshots and daily execution/queue proxies are kept;
no PIT/OOS/live claim. Analysis source and input hashes are saved with output.

Primary output index (generated only by later explicit --execute)

terminal_NAV_delta with both stock and filled-event exact conservation flags.

exposure, risk, cash and ACT, on the exact own-period prior NAV.
{window}_{mode}_RISK_CASH_DAILY.json and _HELD_INDUSTRY_RISK_DAILY.json:
current held/budget/pool risk and actual cash bridge; sparse industry risk rows
mean omitted industries have zero held positions that close.
{window}_{mode}_ALL90_INDUSTRY_PERIODS.json and _ALL1709_STOCK_PERIODS.json:
zero-inclusive industry/stock wealth, exposure, fills, cash and carry inventory.
SEMICONDUCTOR_ALL126_H1_PARTICIPATION.json / SEMICONDUCTOR_H1_COMPARISON.json:
all126 H1 evaluation and exact industry/exposure checks, not the old25 subset.
{window}_{mode}_DIRECT_CONFIRMED_CORE_TRAJECTORIES.json:
actual entry, receipt/budget link quality, current I line, actual qty, mark,
cash/dividend, exit proposal/fill, later stock-flat close and later actual buy.

{window}_ALL_FILLED_EVENT_DELTAS.json / _STOCK_DELTAS.json /
_ALL_PERIOD_STOCK_WEALTH_DELTAS.json: complete contribution bridges.
{window}_ORIGINAL_MOBILE_PATH_CHANGES.json / _CHANGED_EVENT_{OFF,ON}_CONTEXT.json:

{window}_ORIGINAL_TOP10_WINNER_RETENTION.json: fixed original winner cohort,
original held-day overlap, own-NAV capital participation and actual outcomes.
SOURCE_SHA256.json / SCOPE.json / NATIVE_DERIVED_EX_MARK_REFERENCES.json:
actual source/data hashes, no new-account claim, native stale-action mark scope.
