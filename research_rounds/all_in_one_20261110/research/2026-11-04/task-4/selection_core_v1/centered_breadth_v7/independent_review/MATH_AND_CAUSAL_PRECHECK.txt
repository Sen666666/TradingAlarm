v7 centered breadth: independent mathematical and data-interface precheck

Scope: proofs and read-only source/interface review only. No new stock data,
feature/rank/label calculation, model, account, parameter test, or new tail
aggregation. Kappa=10 is supplied by the user, not estimated here.

1. Definitions and the decisive queue-equivalence result

At signal CLOSE T, let n be the size of the same current-T industry cohort
used at both endpoints T and T-5; u1/u0 count its members above their own
causally known SMA20 at the corresponding endpoint. Let du=u1-u0, and
dm=m1-m0, where m1 and m0 use the market's own observable pool on each date.

B1prime=(u1+kappa*m1)/(n+kappa)
B0prime_paired_at_T=(u0+kappa*m0)/(n+kappa)
Dshrink=B1prime-B0prime_paired_at_T=(du+kappa*dm)/(n+kappa)
Dcenter=Dshrink-dm=(du-n*dm)/(n+kappa)
       =n/(n+kappa)*((u1/n-u0/n)-dm), for n>0.

The identity requires the SAME n in the two shrinkage endpoints. It does
not require equal n across industries. If endpoint cohorts/denominators
differ, this paired-cohort simplification must not be applied to their
separately computed observed breadth values.

Most importantly, full-market dm is the SAME scalar for all industries on
a date. Hence for every industry pair i,j,
  Dcenter_i-Dcenter_j = Dshrink_i-Dshrink_j.
All pairwise orderings and exact ties, known-set membership and exact
midpercentiles are identical to v6. With unchanged known population,
pR5, fixed weights, tie-breaking and quota, the fixed Tcenter queue is
EXACTLY the v6 T10 queue. This remains true with different industry n.
There is no marginal cross-sectional ranking improvement to test here.
The full-market main-queue results should inherit the already-saved v6
statistics; a fresh tail aggregation cannot establish an incremental effect.
Exact/rational comparison avoids artificial floating-point tie changes.

Centering changes the absolute level and its interpretation. It does not
add independent evidence. Within equal-n groups it also preserves raw
paired-breadth-change order, but that narrower observation is not the
reason the whole v6 queue is unchanged: common-scalar subtraction is.

2. Interpretation and causal clock

Dcenter is a FIVE-SESSION RELATIVE BREADTH CHANGE. It is not a second time
difference or acceleration. A subsequent difference of Dcenter would be a
different descriptor and, with moving cohorts, would not isolate pure
within-industry acceleration either.

du=0 and dm>0 imply Dcenter<0 for n>0. This says that the industry's net
above-MA20 count did not match an improving market baseline. It does not
say that prices did not rise, that no individual member crossed, that
there is no tradable opportunity, or that an entry should be forbidden.
Even positive du can yield negative Dcenter when it lags n*dm.

Both endpoints for the industry use U_T: their historical u0 and paired
B0prime are a T-known retrospective descriptor, not the actual membership
or value published on T-5. The market endpoints deliberately use own-U
on each date. This mismatch is explicit; it must not be silently described
as a fully fixed-cohort market return/diffusion comparison.

All close/amount/liquidity inputs defining U_T and above-MA20 are only
available at their original complete signal-CLOSE clock. No future pivot,
later member list, later effective action, next-day high/low/volume, or
future label can select or repair this row. T evidence cannot authorize
an earlier T OPEN. Existing static1709/current-industry-bridge and
supplier-vintage limitations remain; algebraic causality is not formal PIT
or executable next-OPEN tradeability certification.

3. Correct leave-industry-out (LOO) interface

Full-market current totals are market_up1 / market_n1.
Full-market old totals are market_up0_ownU / market_n0_ownU.
For the target industry let
  n_old=N_U_previous5,
  u_old=old_ownU_up_lag5_recovered.
Then
  m1minus=(market_up1-u1)/(market_n1-n),
  m0minus=(market_up0_ownU-u_old)/(market_n0_ownU-n_old).

The old market total was constructed from historical own-U counts. It
must subtract u_old and n_old. Subtracting the current-cohort paired u0/n
would mix two different historical populations and can produce a wrong
denominator, negative numerator, or an apparently plausible but incorrect
baseline. U-intersection SIZE is not its above-MA20 numerator.

For n>0 the optional LOO sensitivity has
  Dcenter_minus=(du-n*(m1minus-m0minus))/(n+kappa).
The LOO baseline varies by industry, so its queue may differ from v6;
the common-subtraction ranking proof must NOT be extended to LOO.
LOO removes the target industry's direct additive contribution to the
baseline only. It does not make industry and market independent, remove
shared shocks, or fix static selection/classification limitations.

4. Why LOO is not generally a constant rescaling

Write b1=u1/n, b0p=u0/n, b0o=u_old/n_old when n_old>0,
alpha1=n/market_n1, alpha0=n_old/market_n0_ownU, and
r1=m1minus, r0=m0minus. The exact market decomposition is
  m1=alpha1*b1+(1-alpha1)*r1,
  m0=alpha0*b0o+(1-alpha0)*r0.
With w=n/(n+kappa), the general relationship is
  Dcenter_all = (1-alpha1)*Dcenter_minus
      + w*[alpha1*(b0o-b0p)+(alpha0-alpha1)*(b0o-r0)].
This expression uses n_old>0; the direct count formulas remain the proper
interface when an old industry pool is genuinely empty.
Only under the additional conditions alpha0=alpha1=alpha and b0o=b0p
does Dcenter_all=(1-alpha)*Dcenter_minus follow. Moving own-U membership
can break b0o=b0p, and changing market/industry sizes can break equal
alpha. Neither condition should be presumed from a common current n.

5. Required known/empty/invalid statuses

Required exact inputs: date/same T-5 CSI observation, sector ID, n,u1,u0,
market_up1,market_n1,market_up0_ownU,market_n0_ownU, and for LOO
u_old,n_old plus their recovery/source status. Check integer/count bounds,
same-date market totals, disjoint-source population accounting and
one-to-one date/sector keys. Preserve original count provenance.

Missing counts or missing historical price/MA windows remain UNKNOWN,
not zero. For n=0, the industry breadth has no observations: mark it
NO_INDUSTRY_COHORT rather than treating the algebraic zero as evidence.
Market or LOO denominator<=0 is EMPTY_REFERENCE/UNKNOWN, not zero
market movement. Invalid negative remaining counts are INPUT_MISMATCH,
not clipped into range. An actually known empty old industry pool can
contribute known zero counts to a still-nonempty LOO market; absence of
the old source row is not the same case. Unknown required endpoints do
not get an as-of-fill or a changing partial reference without disclosure.

The actual v6 input batch was positive-n and complete90; this is not proof
that general missing/empty branches have been exercised. Any comparison
must preserve known-set semantics; do not fill missing scores with0 or
quietly change the rank-reference population.
