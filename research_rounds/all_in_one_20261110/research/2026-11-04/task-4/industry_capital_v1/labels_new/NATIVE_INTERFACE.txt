Native single-asset net labels: planned interface, not a rotation portfolio

Use the unchanged LR.Account (BP.Account + SectorAccount), original
LR.preview/reserve_quantity/fill_buy/sell/fill_exit and native HistoryRunner
_actions/_payments. This creates isolated single-asset ledger objects and
hypothetical fills, counted explicitly. It creates no strategy Runner, RiskBook,
cash/industry sharing, actual order or portfolio-policy account.

Frozen primary quantity is the dated legal minimum from the real rejected
OPEN's original LR.preview. The preview max is request scale only. A partial
entry is counted at actual hypothetical shares; unfilled shares expire at the
same entry-day CLOSE. Nonfills have no profit label. The q_min shadow is not
proof that the actual industry/cash/risk gates can fund it.

Retain original signal quote/clock, original I floor/factor and fee contract.
Entry budget is the known OPEN gross plus original fee for q_min. The isolated
ledger's seed cash equals that cost; it does not borrow or import portfolio
money. Technical calibration may instead seed the original actual fill qty.

No new CA formula: call original _actions/_payments through a thin harness with
one account and an empty original B0 controller. Dates include native 00:00
effective events. Entry-day action precedes the hypothetical entry; a new buyer
does not receive the prior-holder entitlement. Original split/fraction policy,
bonus sellability, dividends, unknown payment dates and settlement remain native.

CORE exit proposal is the first original I invalid, original source invalid or
frozen original-account ACT off CLOSE. It stays latched regardless of rebound.
Original native sell windows/T+1/FIFO/capacity control actual hypothetical exit.
A declared source discontinuity cannot acquire a reliable numeric return by
silently joining prices; retain unknown economic label even if an exit can be
requested using a last-known identified quote.

Sparse event schedule may omit no-op holds: entry OPEN/CLOSE, CA effective
times, first exit-proposal CLOSE, native OPENs while a sell is pending, and the
common terminal CLOSE. At each processed time, settle_due and original payments
process all already-due cash. No interest or reinvestment exists, so deferring
pure cash settlement/payment until the next event cannot alter a later trade or
terminal NAV. Technical comparisons must verify this versus actual original
single-CORE paths before full labels.

Common endpoint is the fully completed exit OPEN's same-day CLOSE; proceeds
stay cash until it. If not exited by the original window's final CLOSE, preserve
censoring, gross native NAV and a separately estimated net liquidation value.
That estimate is not an executed trade. Unreliable source/unsupported interfaces
stay unknown, never removed from the mother.

First technical stage only: 3-5 actual closed CORE paths with no other same-sid
buy layer during the holding interval. Include native CA if an eligible path
exists. Compare entry/exit fills, prices, fees, CA entitlement/stock units and
terminal wealth. Technical selection does not filter the later rejected-event
mother. Do not run all labels until root + independent review.
